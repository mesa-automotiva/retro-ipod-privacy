Frequency Generator CLOF22 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Frequency Generator CLOF22 (package com.dialody.player) is an offline tone generator for Android. It plays a sine tone at a frequency chosen by the user. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

No data collection

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal or device data. It does not require an account, does not use the internet and requests no Android permissions.

Data kept on your device

The last frequency and volume you used are saved only on your device so the app opens with the same settings. You can remove this information by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

No advertising and no purchases

The app shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and offers no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children

The app is not directed specifically to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features change. The effective date above will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support details on the app's Google Play listing or open an issue at the privacy support page.
